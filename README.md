### What I build

**Go backend & infrastructure**  
Designing and building services in Go: REST and gRPC APIs, concurrency, background workers, message-driven systems, databases, caching, external integrations, and infrastructure that is observable and maintainable.

**Solana & Web3 engineering**  
Rust/Anchor programs, account models, PDAs, SPL-token flows, transaction integrations, and off-chain services such as keepers and monitoring tools.

**Rust & Python tooling**  
Systems-oriented components, automation, data processing, and utilities that connect different parts of a product.

### Engineering mindset

I care about the parts of software that are easy to overlook: failure paths, data consistency, authorization boundaries, concurrency, repeatable builds, and testing beyond the happy path.

**Design for failure. Test assumptions. Ship with evidence.**
